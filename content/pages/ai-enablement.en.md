Eastbright Global’s AI Enablement puts AI in your team’s hands: it maps your workflows, sets up how people and AI work together, builds your brand knowledge base and trains your team. Tools change. The capability stays.

## What does this service include?

- Marketing workflow diagnostic: map processes and roles to find where AI adds the most value
- AI collaboration and review process: clear workflows and quality standards for working with AI
- Brand knowledge base: brand voice, product details and past work in one place
- In-house training and adoption support: training pitched at your team’s level, with hands-on support

## Why do tech brands need this service?

AI makes output cheap, but producing the right output depends on the team’s judgement and process. Eastbright Global markets AI products itself and turns what works into practical workflows and training, so AI becomes part of how the team works rather than a one-off tool.

## What role does AI play in this service?

This service is AI adoption itself. Every workflow keeps decision points with people, and anything involving sensitive information is handled by people.

## Which markets does this service cover?

The service covers Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macau and Southeast Asia, with teams in Taiwan and Kuala Lumpur (GMT+8) that test and validate directly in market.

## How do you start working with Eastbright Global?

Every engagement starts with a consultation. Brands still deciding whether to enter, or where, can start with the 7-working-day, USD 300 [Brand Check](../index.html#diagnostic). Project and retainer fees are quoted by scope during the consultation.

## FAQ

### Which teams is AI Enablement for?

It suits marketing teams that want to adopt AI while keeping quality and brand consistency, including AI, SaaS and tech brands expanding into new markets.

### How is brand data protected when introducing AI?

Eastbright Global is happy to sign an NDA before work begins. Brand data is used only for the client’s project, AI tools are used in line with the client’s data policy, and anything sensitive is handled by people.
