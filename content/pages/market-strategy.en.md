Eastbright Global’s Market Strategy Diagnostic helps AI, SaaS and tech brands decide which market to enter and with what angle. It covers market selection and feasibility, research into how competitors communicate locally, and a positioning that local buyers understand and act on.

## What does this service include?

- Market selection and feasibility assessment
- Competitor analysis and messaging research
- Positioning and local messaging strategy
- Brand messaging framework and local tone of voice

## Why do tech brands need this service?

A tech product’s value is rarely hard to explain. The hard part is explaining it differently from competitors in a new market. The Market Strategy Diagnostic studies what competitors say in your target market and what actually resonates, then finds the angle that sets your brand apart.

## What role does AI play in this service?

AI-assisted analysis of competitor messaging and market signals widens the research. The final market judgement and positioning recommendation come from Eastbright Global’s consultants, based on local experience.

## Which markets does this service cover?

The service covers Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macau and Southeast Asia, with teams in Taiwan and Kuala Lumpur (GMT+8) that test and validate directly in market.

## How do you start working with Eastbright Global?

Every engagement starts with a consultation. Brands still deciding whether to enter, or where, can start with the 7-working-day, USD 300 [Brand Check](../index.html#diagnostic). Project and retainer fees are quoted by scope during the consultation.

## FAQ

### How is the Market Strategy Diagnostic different from the Brand Check?

The Brand Check is a 7-working-day, USD 300 starter offer that answers whether to enter and where to start. It delivers market priorities, competitor messaging, localisation risks, a recommended next step, red flags and a live online debrief. The Market Strategy Diagnostic is a fuller strategy engagement, quoted by scope.

### Which brands is the Market Strategy Diagnostic for?

It suits AI, SaaS and tech brands that are established at home and preparing to enter Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macau or Southeast Asia, but are not yet sure about market order or messaging angle.
