FAQ

Getting Started with Eastbright

Don’t see your question? Contact us directly →

01What services does Eastbright Global offer, and where should my brand start?

We offer four core services: Market Strategy Diagnostic, Social & Content Marketing, Meta / Google Advertising and Creator Partnerships. Where to start depends on where you are:

  • Still deciding whether to enter, or where? Start with the Brand Check: 7 working days, USD 300, fully credited if you continue within three months.
  • Market chosen, ready to launch or reposition? A Market-Entry Project covers your entry strategy and first campaigns on a clear timeline.
  • Building a long-term presence? An Advisory Retainer keeps social, ads and creator work running, with AI enablement included.

Project and retainer fees depend on scope and are quoted after a consultation. Not sure which fits? Get in touch.

02How long does it usually take to enter a new market, and how do we begin?
We start with a consultation, then propose a clear scope, timeline and deliverables, and tell you frankly if anything is premature. After launch, ad and conversion metrics typically move within one to two months; word-of-mouth and organic traffic take three to six. We check in monthly and review branded search, share of voice and word-of-mouth every quarter.
03We’ve never marketed overseas. Are we still a good fit?
Yes. It’s the situation we help with most. Our teams in Taiwan and Kuala Lumpur test in market rather than relying on second-hand data. You bring the product knowledge and make the calls; we bring local insight, messaging and creators.
04How do you protect our brand data and trade secrets?
We’re happy to sign an NDA before work begins. Your data is used only for your project and never shared with other clients or third parties. When we introduce AI tools, we follow your data policy, and people, not AI, handle anything sensitive.
05What measurable results can I expect, and how is ROI tracked?

We track results on three levels:

  • Short term: customer acquisition cost, conversion rate and return on ad spend.
  • Medium term: social engagement and word-of-mouth reach.
  • Long term: growth in branded search volume and social share of voice.

Tracking and a dashboard go live from day one, so you can see what every dollar delivers.
