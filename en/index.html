FAQ

Getting Started with Eastbright

Don’t see your question? Contact us directly →

01What services does Eastbright Global offer, and where should my brand start?
Eastbright Global offers four core services: Market Strategy Diagnostic, Overseas Integrated Marketing, Meta / Google Advertising and Overseas KOL/KOC Partnerships. If you are still deciding whether to enter, or which market to enter first, the Cross-Border Brand Check is the best starting point — 7 working days, USD 300, and fully credited if you start a follow-on engagement within three months. If you have chosen your target market and are preparing to launch or reposition, a Market-Entry Project delivers your entry strategy and first wave of activity on a clear timeline. If you want to build a long-term presence in Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macau or Southeast Asia, an Integrated Advisory Retainer keeps overseas integrated marketing, advertising and KOL/KOC partnerships moving, with AI enablement built in. Project and retainer fees are quoted by scope and provided during consultation. Still not sure? We’d be glad to hear from you.
02How long does it usually take to enter a new market, and how do we begin?
Every engagement begins with a consultation. Once we understand your product and target market, we propose a clear scope, timeline and deliverables, and tell you honestly what is still too early. Once execution starts, advertising and conversion metrics typically begin to move within one to two months, while compounding assets such as word-of-mouth and organic traffic generally take three to six months to show a clear upward trend. We hold monthly alignment meetings and review long-term indicators — branded search volume, social share of voice and word-of-mouth — every quarter.
03Our team has no overseas market experience. Are we still a good fit?
Absolutely — this is the situation we help with most often. Eastbright Global’s team is based in Taiwan and Kuala Lumpur, so we test and validate directly in market rather than relying on remote data. You bring the product knowledge and decision-making authority; we provide the local market insight, communication and creator network.
04How are our brand data and trade secrets protected?
We can sign a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) before work begins. Your brand data is used only for your project — never for other clients and never disclosed externally. When introducing AI tools, we adapt to your data policy and keep any steps involving sensitive information in human hands.
05What measurable results can I expect, and how is ROI tracked?
We track results on three levels: in the short term, customer acquisition cost, conversion rate and return on ad spend; in the medium term, social engagement and word-of-mouth reach; and in the long term, growth in branded search volume and social share of voice. We set up the tracking framework and data dashboard at the start, so every marketing dollar maps to clear business results.
