How Is Entering Hong Kong and Macau Different from Entering Taiwan?

By Yvonne Wang · Co-Founder · Growth Marketing Consultant
Published 27 November 2026

Entering Hong Kong and Macau differs from entering Taiwan in three main ways: Hong Kong and Macau each have a second official language (English in Hong Kong, Portuguese in Macau), Hong Kong’s platform mix centres on WhatsApp and WeChat rather than LINE, and both markets are far smaller than Taiwan. All three use Traditional Chinese, but messaging and channel plans that work in Taiwan usually need recalibrating for Hong Kong and Macau.

What do Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan have in common?

Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan share Traditional Chinese script and high digital adoption, which makes all three attractive for digital-first launches. DataReportal’s Digital 2027 reports put the share of the population online in October 2026 at 97.3% in Taiwan, 95.9% in Hong Kong and 89.8% in Macau. Brands can reach most people in all three markets through digital channels.

How do the official languages differ?

The official languages differ in ways that shape everyday brand communication. In Hong Kong, Article 9 of the Basic Law provides that English may also be used as an official language alongside Chinese. In Macau, Article 9 of the Macao Basic Law provides the same status for Portuguese.

For brands, this means Hong Kong campaigns often need to work in both Chinese and English, and bilingual product pages, packaging and customer service are common expectations. Macau shares much of Hong Kong’s media environment but is a separate market with its own consumer base.

How do the social platforms differ between Hong Kong and Taiwan?

The social platforms differ most in messaging. GWI’s Q2 2026 survey of internet users aged 16 and above, published by DataReportal, shows these monthly usage rates:

The table shows three practical differences:

  • Customer messaging needs a different set-up. Taiwan runs on LINE, while Hong Kong runs on WhatsApp and WeChat.
  • Xiaohongshu matters more in Hong Kong. Its monthly use in Hong Kong is 25.3%, against 16.5% in Taiwan.
  • Threads is stronger in Taiwan. Taiwan’s 40.8% monthly use is ten points higher than Hong Kong’s 30.4%.

Facebook, YouTube and Instagram reach similar shares in both markets, so they can anchor paid reach in either.

How big are the Hong Kong and Macau markets?

Hong Kong and Macau are much smaller than Taiwan, which changes how much a brand can spend and test. DataReportal counts 7.06 million internet users in Hong Kong and 650 thousand in Macau in October 2026, against 22.4 million in Taiwan. In Macau, Meta’s advertising tools showed Facebook reaching 322 thousand users and Instagram 257 thousand in late 2026.

Smaller audiences mean campaigns reach saturation faster, so creative needs refreshing more often and budgets should be sized to the audience rather than copied from a larger market.

Should Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan be run as one market?

Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan should not be run as one market, even though they can share product facts, visual identity and some creative assets. Each needs its own messaging, channel plan and creator partners. A practical approach is to keep one brand core across all three and calibrate the local layer for each:

  1. Shared: value proposition, product facts and visual identity.
  2. Calibrated for Hong Kong: bilingual messaging, WhatsApp and WeChat customer contact, and Hong Kong creators.
  3. Calibrated for Macau: budgets sized to a smaller audience and Macau-specific offers and creators.
  4. Calibrated for Taiwan: Taiwanese vocabulary, LINE customer contact and Taiwanese creators.

FAQ

Can a brand use the same Traditional Chinese copy in Taiwan and Hong Kong?

Usually not without changes. Both markets use Traditional Chinese, but vocabulary, tone and references differ, and Hong Kong communication often mixes Chinese and English. Copy normally needs recalibrating for each market.

What is the most used messaging app in Hong Kong?

WhatsApp. In GWI survey data published by DataReportal for Q2 2026, 69.2% of internet users in Hong Kong aged 16 and above used WhatsApp in the past month, ahead of WeChat at 52.3% and LINE at 19.2%.

Is Macau a separate market from Hong Kong?

Yes. Macau shares Traditional Chinese and much of Hong Kong’s media environment, but it has its own official languages, a far smaller audience of about 650 thousand internet users and its own consumers, so it needs its own plan.
