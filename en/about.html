About Eastbright Global: A Cross-Border Marketing Agency for AI and SaaS Brands

Updated 2026-11-27

Eastbright Global is a cross-border marketing agency focused on AI, SaaS and tech brands, helping them expand into international markets through localised marketing strategy, content marketing and creator partnerships. The team is based in Taiwan and Kuala Lumpur and serves Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macau and Southeast Asia.

What services does Eastbright Global offer?

Eastbright Global offers four core services plus AI Enablement:

  • Market Strategy Diagnostic: market selection, competitor messaging research and localised brand strategy.
  • Social & Content Marketing: local community management and word-of-mouth on Threads, Xiaohongshu (RED), Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.
  • Meta / Google Advertising: conversion-focused performance advertising.
  • Creator Partnerships: expert and tech creators (KOLs/KOCs).
  • AI Enablement: helping marketing teams build AI capability they can run on their own.

The starter offer is the Brand Check: USD 300, delivered in 7 working days, and fully credited towards a first project started within three months.

Which brands does Eastbright Global work with?

Eastbright Global focuses on AI, SaaS and tech brands, especially brands preparing to enter Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macau or Southeast Asia, and marketing teams that want to adopt AI. Considered tech purchases need to be understood before they are bought, so Eastbright Global builds trust in new markets through localised messaging, in-depth content and expert creators.
