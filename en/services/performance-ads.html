Meta / Google Advertising

Meta and Google Advertising for AI and SaaS Brands in New Markets

Updated 2026-11-27

Eastbright Global’s Meta / Google Advertising is a conversion-focused service for AI, SaaS and tech brands. Eastbright Global handles strategy, targeting, creative and tracking in-house, uses AI to test creative variants fast, and optimises on the data so every dollar is accountable.

What does this service include?

  • Ad strategy and budget allocation
  • Audience setup and AI creative testing
  • Conversion tracking and data dashboards
  • Performance optimisation and monthly reporting

Why do tech brands need this service?

Advertising is the fastest way to get market feedback when entering a new market. Small tests show which messages, audiences and creative work locally before the budget grows.

What role does AI play in this service?

AI generates creative variants and helps read performance data, so tests run faster with more variants. Which versions to scale or stop is decided by the responsible consultant, based on the data.

Which markets does this service cover?

The service covers Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macau and Southeast Asia, with teams in Taiwan and Kuala Lumpur (GMT+8) that test and validate directly in market.

How do you start working with Eastbright Global?

Every engagement starts with a consultation. Brands still deciding whether to enter, or where, can start with the 7-working-day, USD 300 Brand Check. Project and retainer fees are quoted by scope during the consultation.

FAQ

How soon do ad results show?

Eastbright Global typically sees advertising and conversion metrics start to move within one to two months.

How is ad performance tracked?

Conversion tracking and a data dashboard are set up at the start, with monthly reporting on customer acquisition cost, conversion rate and return on ad spend.
