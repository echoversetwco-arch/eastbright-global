Creator Partnerships

Creator Partnerships for AI and SaaS Brands: Expert and Tech Creators

Updated 2026-11-27

Eastbright Global’s Creator Partnerships pair brands with expert creators (KOLs/KOCs) who know their industry. For products people research before buying, in-depth content builds trust that reach alone cannot, and turns attention into customers.

What does this service include?

  • Creator screening and matching
  • Campaign planning and content scripts
  • Affiliate programmes and co-branded campaigns
  • Reach and performance tracking

Why do tech brands need this service?

AI and SaaS products usually need to be understood before they are bought. Expert and tech creators can turn features into use cases their audience cares about, which builds more trust than exposure alone. Eastbright Global’s creator network includes 2,000+ expert and tech creators.

What role does AI play in this service?

AI-assisted creator–audience matching and buzz tracking help find creators whose audiences match the brand’s buyers. The final choice of creators and content direction is made by the consultant together with the brand.

Which markets does this service cover?

The service covers Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macau and Southeast Asia, with teams in Taiwan and Kuala Lumpur (GMT+8) that test and validate directly in market.

How do you start working with Eastbright Global?

Every engagement starts with a consultation. Brands still deciding whether to enter, or where, can start with the 7-working-day, USD 300 Brand Check. Project and retainer fees are quoted by scope during the consultation.

FAQ

How large is Eastbright Global’s creator network?

Eastbright Global works with a network of 2,000+ expert and tech creators.

Why should tech brands work with expert creators?

Considered purchases need to be understood. Expert creators explain a product’s value through in-depth content, which builds more trust than exposure alone and turns attention into customers.
