Social & Content Marketing

Social and Content Marketing for AI and SaaS Brands Entering Asia

Updated 2026-11-27

Eastbright Global’s Social & Content Marketing builds localised content and word-of-mouth that get AI, SaaS and tech brands seen, talked about and chosen in their target markets.

What does this service include?

  • Threads community management
  • Word-of-mouth on Xiaohongshu (RED) and other platforms
  • Community management on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok
  • Social listening, sentiment monitoring and PR coverage

Why do tech brands need this service?

Every market runs on a different platform mix. Taiwan centres on LINE, Facebook and Threads, while Hong Kong leans more on WhatsApp and Xiaohongshu (see How Is Entering Hong Kong and Macau Different from Entering Taiwan?). Social & Content Marketing picks the right platforms for each market and builds content in the local voice, rather than translating posts from the home market.

What role does AI play in this service?

AI speeds up localised content production and trend tracking, so the team can respond to local conversations faster. Content direction, brand voice and pre-publication review stay with people.

Which markets does this service cover?

The service covers Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macau and Southeast Asia, with teams in Taiwan and Kuala Lumpur (GMT+8) that test and validate directly in market.

How do you start working with Eastbright Global?

Every engagement starts with a consultation. Brands still deciding whether to enter, or where, can start with the 7-working-day, USD 300 Brand Check. Project and retainer fees are quoted by scope during the consultation.

FAQ

Which platforms does Social & Content Marketing cover?

It covers Threads, Xiaohongshu (RED), Facebook, Instagram and TikTok, with the mix and weighting set by how people use platforms in each target market.

Why do tech brands need local social media?

Buying decisions for tech products usually depend on trust and word-of-mouth. Local community management gets the brand talked about in the target market and makes it the first choice that comes to mind when buyers compare options.
