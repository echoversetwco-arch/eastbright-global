Going Global: Which Market Should You Enter First?

Choose the wrong order, and strong execution only amplifies the mistake faster. Our first deliverable is usually a market-priority recommendation — including the markets we advise against for now, and why.

Insight

Cross-Border Marketing: What Carries Over, and What Must Be Rebuilt?

A product’s value proposition can usually be shared across markets; its messaging almost always needs rewriting. Knowing where that line sits is the most cost-effective step in cross-border marketing.

Insight

How Is Entering Hong Kong & Macau Different from Entering Taiwan?

Both use Traditional Chinese, yet Hong Kong and Macau are bilingual, faster-paced cities with higher brand sensitivity. The messaging and channel mix that work in Taiwan often need recalibrating.

Insight

AI Makes Output Cheap. Why Does Judgement Become More Valuable?

When every team can produce ten creative versions a day, the bottleneck moves from capacity to knowing which version to run. We put AI into production and keep people at the decision points.
